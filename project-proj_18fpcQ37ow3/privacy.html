Privacy and Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Information stored on your device

The scorekeeper saves player names, scores and game settings in your browser’s local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it by resetting the scorekeeper or clearing this site’s browser data.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalise advertising.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Consent choices

Visitors in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Switzerland will be shown Google’s consent message where required. You can accept, reject or manage available advertising choices through that message.

Technical information

Our hosting and advertising providers may process basic technical information such as IP address, device type, browser type, referring page and request time for security, delivery, analytics and advertising purposes.

External services

The website may retrieve playing-card images and other resources from third-party content delivery services. Those services may receive standard connection information when your browser requests a resource.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking some storage may prevent saved scores or advertising preferences from working as expected.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@switchcardgame.co.uk.
